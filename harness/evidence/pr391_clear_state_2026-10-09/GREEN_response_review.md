This supports value noninterference for these runs only. The captured-stage JVP/FD checks are selected-layer `qv`, `qc`, `T`, `nc_volume`, `nccn_volume`; the full-runtime JVP/FD checks use the unchanged, unobserved 20-second map but compare only selected-layer `[qv, qc, T, nc_dry_specific, nccn_dry_specific]`. The local epsilon pairs retain their activation gate; the report correctly says this does not certify behavior through the clear/cloud boundary or all internal branches.

The central-difference vector's qv/qc/T components agree much more closely.

Their combined stage number rises by exactly `1.0×10⁸ m⁻³` (within rounding), so the evidence properly makes no universal number-conservation claim. The reported qv/qc and latent-temperature closure checks are explicitly local transfer checks. The 20/10/5-second fixed-forcing results have different qc and activation sequences; they show substep-partition sensitivity in this operator. The report does not infer timestep order or attribute the difference to host dynamics.
