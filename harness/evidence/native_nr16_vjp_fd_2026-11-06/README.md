## Independent NR16 endpoint comparisons

The replay uses the actual represented vector `x₊−x₋` from every stored f64 endpoint and compares `gᵀ(x₊−x₋)` with the **NR16** output difference. It reports both `|observed−predicted|/max(1,|predicted|)` (the parent style) and the signal-relative `|observed−predicted|/max(|observed|,|predicted|,output ULP)`, because several NR16 gaps are below one numeric unit. It also records one-sided gaps and exact `Fraction` input-midpoint offsets. All 26 NR16 output differences exceed **one million output ULP**.

The 18 pairs from #546 and eight earlier QV16/QS16/QG16 pairs produce **19 pairs with unchanged checked cap selections**, **two QS16 pairs with only the recorded masks/substep schedule checked**, and **five branch-crossing finite changes**. All 21 numerical comparisons outside the observed crossings satisfy the parent's existing `5×10^-6` max-one normalized criterion and an additional `5×10^-5` signal-relative guard; their largest signal-relative residual is QC16 at `h=10^-3`, about `1.1171×10^-5`, and decreases roughly as `h²` over the three QC16 steps. The additional guard describes this post-hoc replay and was not preregistered before the saved endpoints. The two QS16 comparisons are numerical agreements with cap status unverified; captured masks and cap choices do not enumerate every runtime branch.

The five separately classified cap crossings are TH16 `h=0.01 K`, QG17 `h=10^-3`, BRS17 `h=10^-3`, QV16 `h=10^-3` and QG16 `h=10^-5`. **A cap crossing does not imply the NR16 secant must be inaccurate:** TH16 and QV16 crossing pairs remain close to their baseline VJP projection in NR16, while QG17/BRS17/QG16 crossing pairs differ substantially. None is accepted as a fixed-branch local derivative check. For the paired QG17+BRS17 relative direction at `h=10^-3`, the observed NR16 difference is `−0.05558594425542651` against VJP prediction `−0.05558592751027125` (signal-relative error `3.0125×10^-7`); the recorded entry density ratio stays below 900.
