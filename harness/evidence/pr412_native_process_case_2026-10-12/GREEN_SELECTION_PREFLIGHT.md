## Findings resolved before running the scan

The initial predicate was too strict (every threshold-QC layer warm), derived finite checks were incomplete, and the interface dimension check was implicit. These were corrected before execution: the rule now requires at least one joint QC-threshold/warm layer, derived `p`, `PII`, `th`, and `T` are finite-checked, and the source must have 39 mass and 40 interface levels. The plan calls its count `finite_screened` and explicitly says this is not complete DSD/moment-admissibility certification. It binds the exact retained forecast path, background valid time and center to the prior PR411 intake and case, verifies the completed run gate, pins plan/selector/source hashes before and after the bounded read, and checks forecast size/mtime stability. At preflight time, the private destination was mode `0700` and both one-time output paths were absent.

The selected-column summary now exposes both the number of joint warm/QC-threshold layers and their minimum temperature, alongside the minimum across all threshold-QC layers; the latter can be below 273.15 K for an eligible column and is separately named. The private slice stores QC and derived-temperature arrays plus the column-level warm mask. Distance is explicitly defined as spherical great-circle Haversine with radius 6,371,000 m, not ellipsoidal geodesy or observed pointing accuracy; sorting is deterministic by `(distance,j,i)`.

The historical intake has no whole-forecast SHA (`forecast_sha256` is null). This scan binds the completed-run receipts, exact forecast path, size/mtime and selected-slice hashes, and rechecks size/mtime after read; it does not claim a whole-file content digest.
