## Coverage and evidence

The result is fixed-candidate descriptive evidence: it does not choose another cell or pixel based on residuals. The native patch is a hyperslab extraction from the saved valid run, not a new forecast execution; the result records zero M/H/optimizer calls and no retry.

The V3 native result has 27 cell/frame diagnostics. Raw `QCLOUD`, `QNCLOUD`, and the five hydrometeor mixing-ratio fields are zero at all 27 samples. This does not mean every number field is zero: `QNCCN` is nonzero. The maximum phase-aware KDM6 `qv/qs` diagnostic spans 95.146–95.988% across the selected samples, with its maximum at bottom-up level 3. The report correctly labels this a KDM6 thermodynamic diagnostic, not observed vapor-pressure relative humidity. The stored unweighted QC sum is not LWP, a vertical path integral, or water closure; the retained dry-mass measure is not used to claim a mass-weighted path.

The AMI patch contains 25 samples in each of seven channels (175 total). All reported DQFs are zero, with no fill pixels or invalid calibrated radiances. The center sample reproduces the retained candidate BTs and DQFs. For IR105, the patch BT range is 288.932–293.708 K, a spatial spread across the observed 5×5 samples. The report contrasts that spread with the earlier simulated-H IR105 temporal change across three saved times (0.00702 K at fixed geometry) and the viewing-center geometry change (0.000295 K at the earlier time). These are different comparisons over different supports: the observed patch spread is not a residual against each pixel and does not establish that one spatially matched model column has that error. Per-pixel radiance comes from the existing reader's hash-bound packed-DN calibration; the patch does not average BT then convert the mean to radiance. These pixel-center samples and their scatter do not define an instrument footprint or calibrated observation-error covariance; the retained 2025 FD SRF status is unverified.

The VIIRS filter selects 103 geolocation centers inside the native-center bounding box, including the fixed candidate. The raw categories are CloudPhase 1 and CloudType 2 for all 103 selected centers. The raw CloudPhaseFlag bytes are 0 for 94 and 5 for 9. The separate QA audit confirms declared `valid_range=[0,1]`, preserves the raw values without bit decoding, and records the nine out-of-range bytes as an unresolved metadata discrepancy. The existing product-family interpretation for raw zero does not extend approval to all 103 samples; the nine raw fives remain unresolved. Neither the category counts nor flag counts establish area cloud fraction, independent observations, or an area-weighted statistic. No full CloudHeight source was available and verified, so no height was extended across the patch.

The native `qv/qs` measure, satellite categories, and raw phase flags remain distinct quantities. Same-event frames and repeated run variants do not add independent cases.
