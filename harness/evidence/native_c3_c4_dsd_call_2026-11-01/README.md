# Native one-call C3/C4 and final DSD evidence

This packet retains the selected raw words from an **executed mp137 host step** at the native column `(164,242)`, one-based levels 21 and 23. The 20 s, one-MPI-rank, one-thread run starts at `2025-07-19 00:00:40` and ends at `00:01:00`. The initial restart, namelist and 79 external input assets passed their historical SHA-256 pins. The historical executable alone reproduced the retained endpoint. A separate diagnostic DSO was built from the pinned historical factorial/actual-QR physics archive with only runtime, coordinator and bridge objects replaced for capture. Its capture-off **and capture-on** full-domain endpoints both have the same SHA-256 as the historical endpoint: `950bc5d67dc8cf4980091ecac19a9b7b1ba94756e83d9bd101180e55863c0bb6`.

The WRF run has two physical tiles. The selected second tile has local `I=2..233`, `J=143..281`, and 39 levels, yielding tensor shape `B=32248`, `K=39`; native `(164,242)` maps to zero-based batch `22617` and native levels 21/23 to zero-based K `20/22`. This mapping was checked independently: the host's selected-column `MP_INPUT` f32 words for QI, NI, P and DEN match the C ABI ingress words at both levels, and six host QI/P/DEN values across two levels identify batch 22617 uniquely among all captured converted-ingress columns. Host `MP_OUTPUT` QI and NI words match the f32 writeback of the C++ final DSD output.

The [selected packet](selected_packet.json) preserves 172 selected scalar rows with dtype and raw IEEE bits, `dtcld`, the two selected host input/output trace records, the build receipt, and hashes for both complete C++ captures, both host traces, the inputs, executable, DSO and endpoints. [The verifier](verify_packet.py) hashes the exact packet bytes before parsing, checks the local executed-overlay source snapshots, binds every selected row to the capture's full `(one_step_call,runtime_call,substep,B,K)` identity, and requires ABI `f32`, working numeric `f64`, and branch `bool` values. It compares raw input/output words and independently replays the C3 ice-nucleation and C4 ice deposition candidates from the consumed operands. The full private captures, checkpoint, executable, input assets and archive are pinned but not redistributed, so their hashes are execution attestations rather than independently downloadable artifacts.

The packet's [historical public sources](public_sources/) are retained separately from the compiled [capture overlay](executed_overlay/). The verifier checks each public source snapshot against its original run receipt, rather than against the current development checkout. In particular, a later edit to `libtorch/src/coordinator.cpp` cannot invalidate this historical replay. A changed snapshot still fails its original digest.

At level 23, 20 s of `ninud` adds `3254.872046257864`, while `nsaut` removes `16.392330196368354`, leaving the recorded NI increase `3238.479716061498`. The QI rate sum contains C3 nucleation and C4 deposition, less `psaut`; level 21 also has a small `piacw` term. Reclassification, saturation adjustment and cleanup occur before final DSD; QI and NI are bit-identical at the two captured endpoints of that interval in these selected cells. The final DSD leaves both QI and NI unchanged at levels 21 and 23; its ice slopes are approximately `11612.32` and `80718.02`, strictly inside the `9080..1.82e6` limits. The host `MP_OUTPUT` stores the corresponding f32 values. Exact output replay does not independently authenticate every limited-rate bit: a sub-ULP rate change can round to the same output. The private capture hashes attest those operands; physical number budgets and the raw QNICE unit basis remain separate checks.

**Coordinate and precision limit:** host QNICE is a REAL4 input. In this call its NI word is widened without a numerical density conversion before sedimentation; later C3 consumes the post-sedimentation working NI. That establishes the executed numerical handoff, not the physical mass/volume basis of QNICE. The C++ working state is f64 and writes REAL4 host outputs. This is value-only forward evidence, not a host JVP/VJP or operational f32-internal parity test. `scientific_status: INCONCLUSIVE` remains appropriate.
