# S10 entry graupel-volume initialization candidate

**S10 remains OPEN.** This default-off mp237 experiment initializes `brs=qg/400` only when `qg>0` and **post-clamp** `brs=0` before ProgB site 1 in the first subcycle of each `kdm62D` call. The source clamps negative `brs` to zero earlier, so these records do not prove raw host `bg=0`. The candidate uses the source-declared but previously unused `rho_mid=400 kg m⁻³`. This is a prescribed closure for a missing post-clamp volume, not a density inferred from the host state. It affects both active and trace graupel; the production default is unchanged.

The generator composes the prior midpoint-plus-exact-zero-rate hybrid, adds one removable entry block, and leaves its four guarded quotient consumers unchanged. Removing the new block restores the prior hybrid source text exactly. Macro-ON/OFF Fortran preprocessing, syntax checks, and object builds with `-ffp-contract=off` passed. Preprocessed `.f90` byte identity is not claimed.

Two early launcher attempts stopped **before WRF model initialization** because MPI selected a Tailscale route. They produced no RSL or history and are not physics results. A compiled `MPI_Init` smoke passed with PRRTE and PMIx restricted to the local `en8` interface. The final one-rank 20-second candidate pair used that same local interface; live socket inspection showed both MPI endpoints on this host's `en8` address, without a Tailscale connection.

The final logging-OFF and logging-ON runs used the same freshly linked executable and retained inputs. Both completed with frames at 0 and 20 seconds. Their history SHA-256 is identical (`55d7162e…bc505c`), both frames pass strict raw-bit comparison, and each history has 253 finite numeric variables over 158,946,410 checked cells. The ON run recorded **24 selected** `S10ENTRY` initializations: 19 active cells and 5 positive trace cells. The [selected REAL4 rows](S10_entry_rho_init_en8_run1/S10ENTRY_selected_rows.txt) permit direct replay of `brs=qg/400`; they are not a whole-domain count. It also recorded 135 `S10HYACT` actions and no `S10HYFAIL` event. The [path-redacted receipt](S10_entry_rho_init_en8_run1/receipt.json) pins the source, build, inputs and completed pair.

Relative to the **different-executable** prior hybrid, the initial frame matches raw-bit. At 20 seconds, 23 of 254 common variables differ; `FOGFRAC_SFC` is the first reported variable, differing in 6 of 65,988 cells with maximum absolute difference 0.1466. This is an intended between-variant measurement, not a parity failure or proof of improved forecast skill. The selected quotient-action keys and action counts remain the same, while 20 of 24 `pgmlt` rows change numerically.

The experiment shows that the entry volume rule is executable and materially changes the forecast. It does **not** establish that 400 kg m⁻³ is the right physical initialization, close the full mass–volume–heat budget, reconcile the number basis, prove native seven-call port parity or AD, or make liquid observation cost acceptable. Those S10 and observation gates remain open.
