## Return-branch mathematics and raw masks

C++ measures `N_in`, `N_out`, `Delta=N_out-N_in`, rho_dry and the exact
`Delta==0` bit **inside the fp64 runtime before division**. Logger reads are
scoped value-only under NoGradGuard, leave all live tensors unchanged, and end
before the original where uses those live tensors. The [isolated trace patch](../nccn_zero_return_mask_trace.patch)
adds no ABI field and is not adopted by main.

The [raw mask packet](supported_native_nccn_masks_2026-10-03.tsv.gz) contains
exact hexadecimal binary64 values, five calls × 39 levels: BASE, mixed plus,
mixed minus, NN plus, NN minus. Each call has 18 zero-delta and 21 nonzero-delta
levels; all five masks are identical. Nonzero-delta levels are one-based 11–18
and 27–39. These are the direct-arm levels, **not branch changes between calls**.
Return selection is branch-local for these two tested directions at all 39
levels. Upstream activation, evaporation, substeps and other KDM process
branches are not certified by these masks.

At exact zero, `n_in+Delta/rho_d` preserves the no-op value while retaining the
live tangent. At representably nonzero delta, direct division is used. The
[independent replayer](../replay_supported_native_nccn.py) checks raw dry density
against entry qv/forcing, delta arithmetic, every mask and the dry-return bits.
All 195 outputs reconstruct exactly. Eight exact-zero outputs differ in bits
from unnecessary direct multiply/divide return; the stable mask coverage and
unchanged FD gates explain the intended arithmetic distinction. Quantization
at neighboring values remains a documented limit; no all-branch smoothness
claim is made.
