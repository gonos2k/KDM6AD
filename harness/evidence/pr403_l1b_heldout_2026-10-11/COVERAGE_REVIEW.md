They are observation inputs for future held-out runs. The current July 19 native case is a different date and its original IC/BC provider lineage is not identified by the inspected receipt, so a new native event cannot be built by attaching held-out BT to it.

No retry/rollback algorithm has been implemented. Preserve failure stage and missing outputs; do not call exceptions successful backtracking.

The present scope can require a reproducible, declared-budget analysis with saved state/gradient and explicit numerical diagnostics without silently claiming convergence. Any review must still report the criterion result and the uncertainty in the stopping status.
