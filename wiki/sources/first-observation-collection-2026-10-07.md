parallel, with all candidate phases unknown pending science QA.

correspondence. The retained native model ends at 00:00:40 while the new
EarthCARE/AMI candidate is around 05:34. No time-matched model, calibrated error,
independent performance or scientific cloud-phase validation is claimed.
External model/reanalysis profiles were not acquired or substituted.
