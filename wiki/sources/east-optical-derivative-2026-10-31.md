# East optical derivative evidence

The [captured packet and verifier](../../harness/evidence/east_optical_derivative_2026-10-31/README.md) cover the frozen ten-profile GG/PP serialized-optical experiment: 48 direct-only perturbations and two combined direct/K cases. Replaying the archived arrays gives bit-identical KMA baseline BTs, nonzero output differences for all 70 cells at each tested direction and step, and a maximum absolute FD–JVP residual of 6.995e-9 at h=1e-4. Residuals depend on step size; a strong plateau is not established. At h=1e-3, all 42 active rows per corner in diameter and mixed perturbations cross the 20 µm Baum-table knot, a plausible but unproven explanation for the larger discrepancy; smaller steps do not cross that knot. Replay checks zero raw quality for all 50 direct and two K outputs and reconstructs native ice-slot K using the saved coordinate factor. Same-run KMA conversion and the saved factor are attributed to the pinned runner; raw captures were hashed after execution. The algebraic transpose of one K array is not an independent VJP. These observations do not certify host-boundary or QNICE derivatives, units, branch behavior, or loaded external libraries. [Korean report](../../docs/NATIVE_EAST_OPTICAL_DERIVATIVE_20261031.md)
