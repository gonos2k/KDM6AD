The [actual LA read](../../harness/evidence/REPORT_LA_decode_2026-10-07.md)
decodes the nine unchanged public originals to 250,000 pixels. Independent
DN/DQF, log1p BT and ellipsoidal forward-grid checks pass. Original zero-based
pixel IDs are preserved while NMSC scan coordinates use a one-based origin.

The reader has separate OBT and planned-UTC metadata and deliberately leaves
`ObsPayload.valid_time_utc` unset. File/table identity is checked across passes.
This closes a bounded R2 decoder step, not independent phase/UTC/footprint/model
correspondence or a calibrated observation policy.
