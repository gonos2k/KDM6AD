# East optical content–diameter factorial

Four actual direct RTTOV calls swapped only serialized ice content and effective diameter at ten east-edge fixed-M0 profiles: GG (guard/guard), PG (pilot content/guard diameter), GP (guard content/pilot diameter), PP (pilot/pilot). GG and PP reproduced historical saved KMA BT for all 70 cells each. PG−GG lowered all 70 BT by 0.7983–5.3701 K and raised their Huber numerator by 123.92018052340481; GP=GG and PP=PG in saved KMA BT and Huber, with zero 2×2 interaction. Raw RTTOV TOTAL did differ by at most 1.42e−14 in the diameter contrasts, so the zero result is specific to saved KMA BT and this precision. Two earlier failed GG attempts are excluded. The experiment is an observation-operator intervention under the retained cloud-fraction gate, not a new physical host state, a boundary budget or a derivative proof.
