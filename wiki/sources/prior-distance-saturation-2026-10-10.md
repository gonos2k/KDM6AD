# Prior distance to a warm saturation boundary

The [bounded analysis](../../harness/evidence/REPORT_main_b679_prior_boundary_2026-10-10.md)
uses three public receipts and the declared theta .8 K/qv log .08 prior. Saved
seven-channel Jo+Jb is higher than baseline at −.5 K and lower at −.8 K. These
three samples are nonmonotonic; they do not prove a full-control cost barrier or
optimizer failure.

At fixed selected-layer pressure, exact qv=qs equality is parameterized by T in
Tb±2 K. The unique bounded minimum has prior .1181830035, compared with cooling
only .4281588809 and moisture only .1630471074. A 20,001-point grid differs by
1.50e-9; independent positive-curvature bounds support uniqueness within that
scalar interval. The result is an onset boundary, not positive supersaturation,
activation, cloud formation, actual optimal analysis, calibrated B or a seed.

main b679 retains the existing full-domain defaults. Do not confuse PR code
